# Android 0.1.0-alpha.47

## Known issues / 已知问题

这是中间测试版。Office 关闭后仍可能保留一份待确认副本，请在恢复入口查看处理。完整升级后，Office／CAD 可选组件需重新安装，原文件保留。手机多步操作、条件计划、跨项目隔离及界面精修仍在推进。

## 更新

- 安装 Office／CAD 组件后重新打开 App，可返回原任务中的文档。
- 修复系统取消被误报为下载或存储失败；保留明确的取消和重试。
- Office 等待组件时不再提前创建编辑副本；下载取消会停止工作并回到文件列表。
- 三星测试候选已验证 STL／DOCX 自动返回及 Office 下载取消；最终发行包和在线升级结果另记。

## English

This intermediate release restores the original task document after Office/CAD installation restarts the App. It distinguishes system cancellation from failures and defers Office edit-copy creation until the component is ready. Download cancellation stops owned work.

Office may still retain an unconfirmed copy after closing; review it in recovery. Optional components need reinstalling after a full upgrade; documents remain. Multi-step phone control, conditional scheduling, cross-project isolation and UI refinement remain in progress. Samsung repair candidates verified STL/DOCX return and Office download cancellation; final release-package and online-upgrade evidence are recorded separately.
