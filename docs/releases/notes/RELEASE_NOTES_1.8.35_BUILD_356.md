# Floe Agent 1.8.35（356）

## 简体中文

在空间或从手记打开化学工程，画出分子后点“三维预览”，即可在设备上查看立体构形。修改二维结构后可重新生成，也可以请工程助手为当前分子生成预览。保存工程后可以重开预览，二维原稿会一起保留。

在空间新建建筑工程，或从“文件”打开一份 .floebuilding 工程。在楼层平面上连续点取墙段、拖动端点，选中墙体后可以改尺寸和两侧饰面。使用“另存为”可保留建筑副本，也可以生成一份独立编辑的 CAD 工程。

新建游戏工程只需名称。进入工程后可以导入已有文件，查看引擎识别依据，或请项目助手协助判断，然后确认引擎和游戏根目录。

聊天记录显示消息的年月日和时间。点自己消息下方的“复制”即可复制整段正文和换行。

串口助手新增 Modbus 操作，可读取线圈、离散输入、保持寄存器和输入寄存器，也可写单个或多个线圈及保持寄存器。连接 Modbus TCP 设备或串口网关后，可在发送前预览报文，并在 RTU、ASCII 和 TCP 封装之间转换。

云端供应商新增 DeepSeek 转写与语音输出。DeepSeek 搜索可按需开启深度思考并查看来源。保存或测试自定义 HTTP 端点时，可以在提示中取消或验证后继续。

图片工作台默认采用单列工具栏，可切换双列。打开“涂抹 → 羽化”，沿边缘局部涂刷。使用 Apple Pencil 时抓手自动收起，手指可以平移和捏合，右下角可调倍率。

手记和 PDF 的钢笔、水彩笔等笔型现在各有图标。普通笔和荧光笔分开选择，各自记住参数。创建、导入 Skill 和继续草稿都在 Skill Hub。

## English

Open a chemistry project from Space or a note, draw a molecule and tap 3D Preview to see its shape on your device. After editing the drawing, generate the preview again or ask the project assistant to show the current molecule. Save and reopen the project to revisit it alongside the original 2D drawing.

Create a building project in Space or open a .floebuilding project from Files. Draw connected walls on the floor plan, drag their endpoints and edit dimensions and finishes on either face. Save an independent building or CAD copy for further editing.

Create a game project with just a name. Inside the project, import existing files, inspect engine detection evidence or ask the project assistant for help before confirming the engine and game folder.

Chats show the date and time of each message. Tap Copy beneath your own message to copy its full text, including line breaks.

The serial assistant adds Modbus operations for reading coils, discrete inputs, holding registers and input registers, and writing single or multiple coils and holding registers. Connect a Modbus TCP device or serial gateway to preview frames and convert between RTU, ASCII and TCP framing before sending.

Cloud providers now include DeepSeek transcription and speech output. Enable thinking search in your DeepSeek search settings to see answers and sources. Saving or testing a custom HTTP endpoint now offers Cancel or Verify and Continue.

The image workbench starts with a single-column toolbar and remembers your layout. Under Smudge, choose Feather to soften an edge with a brush. The Hand tool hides when you use Apple Pencil. Pan or pinch with your fingers, or use the zoom controls at the lower right.

In Notes and PDFs, pens have distinct icons, including the fountain pen and watercolor brush. Pens and highlighters have separate controls and remember their own settings. Create or import a Skill and return to drafts in the Skill Hub.
