# 1.7.23 (264) — Media workbench

Distribution status: see [current status](../../CURRENT_STATUS.md).

## English

- Unified image and video editing from Files, chat attachments and Canvas, with persistent projects, undo/redo, large previews and adaptive iPad/iPhone layouts.
- Image layers, text and drawing, crop/resize/color controls, and verified PNG/JPEG/HEIC exports with native Share / Save to Files.
- Multi-clip primary video track, music and captions, trim/speed/volume, hard cuts and dissolves, with rendered preview and H.264/HEVC exports.
- Revision-bound AI proposals and confirmed candidate insertion reuse configured services. Interrupted or unknown image submissions are not automatically resent. The media.project tool shares project validation and ownership checks.

## 简体中文

- 文件、聊天附件和 Canvas 统一进入图片与视频工作台，支持工程保存、撤销重做、大预览和 iPad／iPhone 自适应布局。
- 图片图层、文字与涂鸦、裁剪／尺寸／调色，以及经过格式验证的 PNG／JPEG／HEIC 导出，可直接分享或存到文件。
- 主视频轨多片段拼接、配乐与字幕、裁切／变速／音量、硬切与叠化，编辑结果参与预览，支持 H.264／HEVC 导出。
- AI 方案绑定工程修订，候选结果确认后加入工程；复用已配置服务，未知或中断的图片提交不会自动重发。media.project 工具复用工程校验和归属检查。
