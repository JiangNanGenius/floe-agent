# Floe Agent 1.7.40 (281)

## 简体中文

- 优化 Office 连续书写、即时笔迹和双指实时缩放，改善画笔开关与软键盘恢复。
- 修复静止落笔的笔点尺寸、轮廓及保存表现，改善取消后续写和手记中的笔迹预览。
- 修复 PPT 保存和导出干扰笔迹撤销、重做的问题。
- 画中画背景音乐新增晨光、深蓝、星河，保留原曲、音量及已保存的设置。

本地编译、模拟器交互与保存文件已按受影响功能核验；真实 Apple Pencil 的压感、防误触及设备手感仍由真机反馈确认。无签名 IPA 需自行签名安装，支持 iOS／iPadOS 26 及以上。

## English

- Improves continuous Office writing, live ink and two-finger zoom, with smoother drawing controls and keyboard recovery.
- Corrects stationary pen-dot size, outlines and saved output, and improves writing after cancellation and ink previews in Notes.
- Fixes PPT saves and exports interfering with ink undo and redo.
- Adds First Light, Deep Blue and Starfield to PiP background music while keeping the original track and saved preferences.

Relevant local builds, Simulator interactions and saved files have been checked. Physical Apple Pencil pressure, palm rejection and device feel remain subject to device feedback. The unsigned IPA requires signing for installation and supports iOS/iPadOS 26+.
