# Floe Agent 1.7.53（294）

## 简体中文

- 改善连续语音的误打断处理，减少回答播放时自行暂停或结束的情况。
- 语音输出先选择播放来源，再选择对应音色。
- 在线语音服务支持直接选用，配置编辑与语音包管理分别保留。
- 切换来源保留各自的音色，以及全局语速和音调设置。
- 普通朗读、试听和连续语音按完整句子合成，减少碎句造成的停顿与语调异常。

- 改善已保存凭据同步的缺失项处理与错误状态恢复。

## English

- Improved false-interruption handling during voice conversations to reduce unintended pauses and cancelled spoken answers.
- Voice output now lets you choose a playback source before selecting its voice.
- Configured online speech services can be selected directly, with separate configuration and voice-pack management.
- Switching sources preserves their voice choices and global speed and pitch settings.
- Read-aloud, previews and voice conversations synthesize complete sentences to reduce unnatural fragment boundaries.
- Improved missing-credential handling and error recovery in saved-credential synchronization.
