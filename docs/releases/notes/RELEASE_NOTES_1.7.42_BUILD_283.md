# Floe Agent 1.7.42 (283)

## 简体中文

- 修复 Office 助手编辑参数问题；手记中的 Office 编辑停顿后自动保存，失败时保留可恢复副本。
- 助手可按工作表、单元格范围、页码或矩形查看文档，包括屏幕外内容；普通发送不再自动附带截图。
- 修复 Excel 荧光笔抬笔后的白底与颜色失真，保留下方文字、网格和底色。
- PDF 导出与打印共用生成前设置：XLSX 可选择工作表或单元格范围、纸张、方向、缩放与页边距；Word／PowerPoint 可选择最终 PDF 页码。
- 助手按文档实际支持的格式导出文件，可直接预览、分享；快捷指令新增“获取运行文件”，返回对应运行的已核验文件。
- 减少手记连续书写和恢复时的误报冲突及重复恢复副本。
- 联网内容插件默认一起启用头条、抖音百科和墨迹天气，无独立来源开关；专业数据集保持独立且默认关闭。

受影响功能已完成本地 App 编译及定向模拟器／保存回读检查。真实 Apple Pencil 压感、防误触及设备手感由真机反馈确认。无签名 IPA 需自行签名安装，支持 iOS／iPadOS 26 及以上。

## English

- Fixes Office assistant edit parameters. Office documents in Notes save after you pause and keep a recoverable copy if saving fails.
- The assistant can inspect sheets, cell ranges, pages and rectangles, including offscreen content. Ordinary messages no longer attach screenshots automatically.
- Fixes white backgrounds and color shifts after lifting the Excel highlighter, preserving the text, grid and fill underneath.
- PDF export and printing share settings before generation: XLSX supports sheet or cell scope, paper, orientation, fit and margins; Word/PowerPoint support final PDF page selection.
- The assistant exports real files in the formats each document supports, with Preview and Share. Shortcuts adds Get Run Files for the verified files of the selected run.
- Reduces false conflicts and duplicate recovery copies during continuous Notes handwriting and recovery.
- Online content enables Toutiao, Douyin encyclopedia and Moji weather together, without separate source switches. Professional datasets remains separate and off by default.

Relevant local App builds and focused Simulator/save-readback checks passed. Physical Apple Pencil pressure, palm rejection and device feel remain subject to device feedback. The unsigned IPA requires signing for installation and supports iOS/iPadOS 26+.
