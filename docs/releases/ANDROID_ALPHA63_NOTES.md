# Android Alpha63

Linux桌面和远程VNC共用查看器增加两种鼠标模式，菜单可切换并记住选择。触控板模式在整个画面相对移动指针，单击在当前指针处左键、双击只发右键；长按后滑动可拖拽。直接触屏继续点哪里操作哪里，保留左键双击、长按右键和拖动。双指滚动／缩放、键盘和隐藏控制保留，取消、切换、后台和关闭会释放输入。

已知问题：固定图形帧在手机竖屏仍有黑边，状态栏对比度及全局UI精修继续。索尼Android9组件失败的具体原因尚未证明；Alpha62新增诊断不代表该设备已修复，不要求先刷机。原图形断连、自动解锁／重新锁屏、部分后台控制与间歇性任务导出仍按原记录推进，整轮N01–N08未结束。旧更新器仍可使用当前完整安装包；不承诺所有旧客户端可自动修复。

The shared Linux/VNC viewer adds persistent Trackpad and Direct touch modes. Trackpad motion is relative across the viewport: one tap clicks at the pointer, two taps send only a right click, and holding then sliding drags. Direct touch retains absolute pointing, left double click, long-press right click and dragging. Two-finger scroll/zoom, keyboard and recoverable fullscreen controls remain available, with cancellation and lifecycle input release.

Known issues: fixed-frame phone letterboxing, status-bar contrast and wider visual refinement remain. The reported Sony Android9 component failure is not diagnosed on that device; Alpha62 diagnostics do not certify a fix or require flashing. The earlier graphics disconnect, unlock/relock, some background control and intermittent transcript export remain open. N01–N08 continues. The full installer remains available for older clients; their installed updater cannot be retroactively fixed.
