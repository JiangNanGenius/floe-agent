## Evidence handling

- Real provider runs used the tester's configured provider through the App's production runtime and registered tools. Keys remain in credential storage; no key, request body or response body is recorded in evidence.
- Retained failures are listed next to the accepted results instead of being relabeled. Independent postprocess parsers re-read the ZIP/XML of the produced documents; they are separate from the product code path being validated.
- The accepted real provider scenarios were reused after material fixes; no paid repetition was performed solely for screenshot polish or for the PPT ordering-only test correction. Earlier attempts and iterations — including the initial provider 404s and the Word iterations before the parser fix — remain recorded rather than erased.
