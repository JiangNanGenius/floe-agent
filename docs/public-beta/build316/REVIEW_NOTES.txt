Floe 1.7.75 (316) — TestFlight update

This update repairs background audio volume restoration, improves the existing local ARM64 runtime, and accepts standard CAD observation view names. It introduces no new permission categories or account requirements.

Background sound is user controlled in Settings > Background Execution. It can be previewed and its volume adjusted independently. After a pause or audio interruption, the current saved volume is applied before the loop resumes. Zero volume remains muted playback.

Floe is free to download. Cloud services use credentials configured by the user; saving settings does not generate paid images or video. Existing project files are retained.

The existing Build315 App Store submission is not replaced by this TestFlight update.
