Floe 1.7.77 (318) — TestFlight update

This update adds low device storage notices and clear recovery guidance when local data cannot open or a write fails because storage is full. Warnings do not automatically delete chats, projects or skills. Device capacity is measured locally and is not sent to a provider.

Terminal startup now belongs to its persistent session owner, so changing between embedded and full-screen presentation does not cancel the connection. Explicit stop and reconnect remain available. The existing local ARM64 runtime and user files are retained; no JIT or new permission category is introduced.

The update includes previous local runtime scheduling and background sound volume restoration fixes. Background sound remains user controlled in Settings > Background Execution, with independent preview and volume controls.

Floe is free to download. Cloud services use credentials configured by the user; saving settings does not generate paid images or video.

This TestFlight update does not replace the existing Build315 App Store submission or alter its automatic-release choice.
