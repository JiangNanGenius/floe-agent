Floe Agent 1.7.46 (Build 287), bundle org.floeagent.ios, is an iPad-first workspace with iPhone support.

No Floe account or demo login is required. Notes, local Office editing and PDF annotation work without cloud AI. Optional AI tasks use the tester's own configured provider; no provider credit is included.

This build focuses on Office print and export. Open a spreadsheet workbook in Notes and use Print or Export PDF. Page setup adds a Print gridlines choice (Document settings, Print, or Don't print), and Scaling adds Exact scale with a typed percentage plus a slider. The real PDF preview under the options regenerates automatically as settings change; please change print options repeatedly, check pagination, then print or share that same previewed output. Saving also works after switching an Office document to read mode; please check save, document close and reopening with the updated cover.

The Skills list now shows origin badges: Official, Internal, External, and Unknown for older records whose source cannot be proven. For scheduled tasks, tapping the Shortcuts badge starts the import directly, and when a plan ends a popup offers cleanup of its Shortcut and automation. Scheduled execution runs only through automations the tester creates and controls in Apple's Shortcuts app; there is no arbitrary background execution.

Adaptive layouts for foldable devices turn on automatically when such hardware is detected and can be turned off in Settings → General; the option never appears on ordinary iPhone and iPad models. Foldable support in this build is code-level and was qualified in software; please report results from your own hardware, as no specific foldable device is claimed as tested. Physical Pencil feel, real printers and other hardware-specific behavior remain part of beta testing. Please use synthetic files and redact private content in feedback. This beta leaves the existing formal App Store submission unchanged.
