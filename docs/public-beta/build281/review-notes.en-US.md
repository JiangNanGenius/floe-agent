Floe Agent 1.7.40 (Build 281), bundle org.floeagent.ios, is an iPad-first workspace with iPhone support.

No Floe account or demo login is required. Notes, local document editing and PDF annotation can be reviewed without configuring cloud AI. Optional cloud AI uses a provider key supplied by the tester; the app includes no provider credit.

This beta focuses on Office ink and zoom, saved Notes previews, and additional background-music choices.

Suggested review:
1. In Notes, create or import a synthetic Word, Excel or PowerPoint document. Enable the pen and draw consecutive strokes and stationary dots.
2. Use two fingers to zoom with the pen enabled and disabled. Save, return to Notes and reopen the document; confirm the original content and ink remain.
3. Try the pen and highlighter, cancel a contact, continue writing, and use undo/redo on completed ink.
4. Optional PiP music has four local tracks and adjustable in-app volume. Increase the music volume for audible playback. Background music, task narration and cues have separate saved controls.

Physical Apple Pencil feel and long-running device behavior are part of beta feedback. Please use synthetic documents and redact private content before sending feedback. This submission does not change the existing formal App Store submission.
