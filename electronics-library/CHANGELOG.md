# Library changes

## 1.0.0 — candidate, unpublished

- 12 categories: passives, diodes/LEDs, transistors/MOSFETs, power, analog/op-amps, digital logic, microcontrollers, connectors, switches/relays, crystals, sensors and modules.
- 47 usable combinations, with original source files, explicit pin/pad maps, full attribution and compatibility notes.
- Three unsupported source combinations remain blocked and recoverable in the catalog; no empty or simplified replacement is substituted.
- Independent KiCad symbol/footprint imports may be explicitly associated into a custom combination. The association preserves both sources and does not assert a manufacturer part number.
- Category content is prepared for the existing signed-content mechanism. Signing, the final source checkpoint, version compatibility and public read-back are release steps that have not yet occurred.

## 1.0.0 — 未发布候选

- 覆盖 12 类、47 个可用组合，保留原始数据、引脚映射、来源和兼容性说明。
- 3 个不支持的来源组合明确阻断，不用简化图形替代。
- 独立导入的符号与封装可经明确映射保存为自定义组合，不宣称厂商型号。
- 分类包准备接入既有内容更新；签名、最终源码版本、App 版本核验及公开读回尚未执行。
