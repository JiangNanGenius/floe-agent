# Fixture provenance

- `psd/` and `ora/` are Floe-authored synthetic qualification fixtures from
  the PSD/ORA research of 2026-11-02, copied byte-for-byte. Their hashes are in `SHA256SUMS`, regenerated with package-relative
  paths; the hashes match the formats package manifest. They contain no user image, brush, font or production media.
- The PSD fixtures were generated by the formats package's scripted fixture
  generator; the ORA fixtures were generated by its Python fixture generator
  and synthetic PNG layers.
- `psd/*_psdtools_composite.png` are independent reference renders from the
  formats qualification evidence, retained read-only for comparison.
- FCPXML and OTIO test documents are authored in the tests as string/JSON
  fixtures; no third-party media is copied.
