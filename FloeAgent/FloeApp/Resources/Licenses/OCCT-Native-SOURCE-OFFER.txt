OCCT 7.8.1 native static libraries - source and relink record
=============================================================

FloeCADKit (and the Floe app that embeds it) links Open CASCADE Technology
(OCCT) version 7.8.1 as static libraries for iOS device and simulator. OCCT is
distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 with the Open
CASCADE exception 1.0; the complete license text and the exception ship with
this app (EngineeringViewers/license.occt.txt and
EngineeringViewers/OCCT_LGPL_EXCEPTION.txt are byte-identical to the copies in
ThirdParty/FloeCADKit).

This page is an engineering record of where the complete corresponding source
is available and which build inputs are retained to rebuild or relink the
linked library. It is not a legal certification and does not replace the
license texts themselves.

Complete source for the exact linked version
--------------------------------------------
OCCT 7.8.1 is unmodified upstream source; Floe modifies no OCCT source file.
  Project:  https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT
  Tag:      V7_8_1 (lightweight tag)
  Commit:   bd2a789f15235755ce4d1a3b07379a2e062fdc2e
  Archive:  https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT/archive/refs/tags/V7_8_1.tar.gz
The source is public and requires no registration, request or contact.

Status and limits
-----------------
- This build links the reviewed upstream-built slices. Floe has not
  independently rebuilt OCCT and claims no byte-identical reproduction of a
  local build.
- The record above is the actual state of this source tree; it is an
  engineering fact sheet, not a legal opinion or certification. The release
  owner performs the final license review before distribution.
