ShioriKrkr corresponding source and relinking
===========================================

Application source tag: v1.8.78
Repository: https://github.com/JiangNanGenius/floe-agent

Floe includes KiriKit 0.1.3 (MPL-2.0) and the KiriKiri core and plug-ins.
The signed ShioriKrkr framework statically incorporates FFmpeg n8.0.3
(LGPL-2.1-or-later). The matching App source tag contains both source archives,
their checksums, pinned engine and plug-in patches, and the procedure to rebuild
both iOS slices and relink the App.

https://github.com/JiangNanGenius/floe-agent/tree/v1.8.78/FloeAgent/ThirdParty/FloeVisualNovelRuntime/Relink

See Relink/README.md for the build commands. After editing the library, rebuild
ShioriKrkr and replace the framework in the matching Floe App source, then build
and sign with your own identity. The applicable license permits modifying the
library and reverse engineering for debugging those modifications. The full
license texts accompany this notice.
