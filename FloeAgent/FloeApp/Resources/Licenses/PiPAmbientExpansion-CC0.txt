PiP ambient meditation loops — additional tracks (bundled audio)

All three tracks are original compositions by Yoiyami, published on
OpenGameArt under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
CC0 requires no attribution; the credits below are the optional courtesy
credits the author suggests ("Music by Yoiyami (CC0)").

1. "First Light Particles – CC0 Atmospheric Piano/Ambient Track"
   Source: https://opengameart.org/node/182244
   Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Bundled file: FloeApp/Resources/Audio/PiPAmbientFirstLight.wav
   Modification: a 66 s excerpt (49–115 s of the author's 131.72 s WAV) was
   stitched into a 60.000 s seamless loop with a 6 s equal-power tail-to-head
   crossfade and converted to 48 kHz stereo 16-bit PCM. Peak 0.900, no clipping.
   The source page describes calm atmospheric piano with ambient pads and
   "no groove, no percussion".

2. "Yoiyami Core Theme – Deep Blue Ambient Piano"
   Source: https://opengameart.org/node/182243
   Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   Bundled file: FloeApp/Resources/Audio/PiPAmbientDeepBlue.wav
   Modification: a 66 s excerpt (64–130 s of the author's 234.96 s WAV) was
   stitched into a 60.000 s seamless loop with a 6 s equal-power tail-to-head
   crossfade and converted to 48 kHz stereo 16-bit PCM. Peak 0.560, no clipping.
   The source page describes soft piano with airy pads and no heavy groove.

3. "Starfield Romance – CC0 Ambient / Emotional Space Theme
    Yoiyami Blue Series – No.3"
   Source: https://opengameart.org/node/182246
   Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
